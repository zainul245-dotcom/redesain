## Brand & Style
The design system bridges multi-generational Indonesian botanical heritage with contemporary global B2B trade infrastructure. Designed for international commodity brokers, specialty food conglomerates, perfumeries, and institutional procurement officers across Europe, North America, and East Asia, the aesthetic communicates absolute transparency, ecological stewardship, and uncompromising luxury.

The visual direction combines **Warm Editorial Minimalism** with **Precision Data Tactility**. It balances the organic warmth of high-grade volcanic soil and dried aromatic spices with the crisp, disciplined architecture of cross-border financial systems. High-definition botanical textures sit alongside translucent glass telemetry cards, batch tracing timelines, and live export analytics. 

Key visual principles:
- **Agronomic Authority:** Unapologetic use of rich, heritage botanical deep greens anchored by warm parchment bases instead of sterile digital whites.
- **Precious Commodity Value:** Amber and warm gold accents are applied strictly as precision data indicators, verification seals, and high-priority points of interaction.
- **Traceability Transparency:** Complex logistics and ESG metrics are structured through layered glass surfaces, monospaced batch stamps, and clean tabular precision.

## Colors
The palette evokes the terroir of Indonesian agroforestry: volcanic dark soil, lush rainforest canopy, sun-cured spices, and unbleached parchment shipping manifests.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#0F2D25` - Deep Forest Green):** The core corporate anchor. Used for primary CTAs, master navigation surfaces, institutional hero banners, and high-impact structural blocks.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` - Spice Amber):** Represents sun-dried cinnamon quills, matured nutmeg arils, and cured vanilla pods. Used for conversion triggers, status highlights, live interactive states, active batch telemetry, and high-tier origin badges.
- **Tertiary (`#081C16` - Deep Maluku Obsidian):** A near-black forest green used for high-contrast deep footer segments, floating commodity headers, and dark-mode data visualizations.
- **Neutral (`#1F2937` - Slate Charcoal):** Delivers clean optical contrast for complex export tables, metric figures, and legal disclaimers without the harshness of pure `#000000`.

### Background & Surface Tones
- **Base Canvas (`#FDFBF7` - Organic Parchment):** The luminous, warm foundation for all page layouts, eliminating cold glare.
- **Subtle Layering (`#F7F3EB` - Warm Sandstone):** Applied to alternate table rows, card backings, container groupings, and passive borders (`#E6E0D2`).
- **Accent Tints:** Gold illumination (`#F59E0B`), deep harvest ochre (`#B45309`), and bio-verification green (`#10B981`) for international certification seals.

## Typography
The typographic system establishes a dynamic contrast between classical agricultural provenance and precise trade logistics.

### Hierarchy & Pairing
- **Playfair Display (Headlines & Editorial Titles):** Imparts old-world maritime prestige, high culinary craftsmanship, and luxury commodity trading history. It is preserved for high-tier section introductions, commodity titles, and value assertions.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (UI, Data, Specs, & Body):** Provides geometric clarity, tall x-height, and neutral precision across enterprise screens. Used for multi-column manifest forms, moisture content metrics, Incoterms selectors, and interactive batch identifiers.

### Typographic Discipline
- **Micro-Copy & Metrics:** All uppercase labels (`label-sm`, `label-md`) must utilize wide letter spacing (0.04em to 0.08em) for instantaneous legibility in shipping manifests and RFQ workflows.
- **Numeric Tabulation:** Quantitative figures in batch trackers, weights, freight pricing, and chemical assay analyses must use tabular numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to align data across columns effortlessly.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout uses an asymmetric 12-column grid system tuned for dense international trade documentation alongside expansive editorial spice showcase layouts.

### Structural Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout, `margin: 1.25rem`, `gutter: 1rem`. Dynamic metrics condense into horizontal swipe carousels.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout, `margin-md: 2.5rem`, `gutter: 1.5rem`. Split views between commodity specification sheets and origin stories.
- **Desktop (> 1024px - 1440px):** 12-column grid, max container width `1360px`, `margin-lg: 5rem`, `gutter-lg: 2rem`. High-density analytics, interactive multi-step quotation engines, and live tracking sidebars coexist without viewport clutter.

### Spacing Philosophy
Negative space is deliberately maintained around editorial headings (`space-xl` and above) to evoke the scale of an upscale auction house, while component-level spacing (`space-xs` through `space-md`) remains compact and modular to streamline procurement workflows.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is constructed through frosted botanical glassmorphism, tonal parchment stacking, and amber-diffused ambient glows rather than standard grey drop shadows.

### Atmospheric Layering
1. **Level 0 (Terroir Bed):** Base background canvas in `#FDFBF7` or rich institutional dark sections in `#081C16`.
2. **Level 1 (Parchment Tile):** Flat `#F7F3EB` surfaces framed by a micro-border of `1px solid rgba(15, 45, 37, 0.08)`. Zero shadow; separation is driven strictly by tonal delta.
3. **Level 2 (Glassmorphic Micro-Cards):** Translucent overlays (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.75)` on light surfaces, or `rgba(15, 45, 37, 0.65)` on dark sections) layered with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a directional top highlight border of `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.4)`. Used for floating batch statistics, chemical purity readouts, and vessel ETA badges.
4. **Level 3 (Amber Interactive / Floating Modules):** Elevated quotation drawers and origin tooltips. Shadows are warm and low-opacity: `box-shadow: 0 16px 32px -8px rgba(15, 45, 37, 0.12), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.08)`.
5. **Glow States (Active Tracking Nodes):** Interactive supply chain map waypoints and active RFQ fields use a focused amber radiation: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.2), 0 0 16px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.35)`.

## Shapes
The design uses refined, tailored **Soft Corners (`roundedness: 1`)**. 

Corners are subtly smoothed (4px base, 8px on cards, 12px on modal surfaces) to maintain the structural discipline of legal trade documents while softening industrial edges. 
- **Data Tables & Technical Blocks:** Precision 4px radius (`rounded-sm`).
- **Interactive Micro-Cards & Quotation Steps:** Balanced 8px radius (`rounded-md`).
- **Pill Exceptions:** Status badges, ESG verifications, and commodity price ticker capsules exclusively deploy full pill contours (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to indicate live, interactive, or verified state indicators.

## Components

### 1. Primary & Secondary Buttons
- **Primary Export Action:** Solid `#0F2D25` container with `#FDFBF7` typography, `rounded-sm` (4px), vertical padding `14px`, horizontal padding `28px`. Hover transition to `#081C16` accompanied by an inner hairline border shimmer of `rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.4)`.
- **Secondary Quote Action:** Transparent surface, `1.5px solid #D97706` border, `#B45309` typography. Hover brings an amber wash (`rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.08)`) with zero layout shift.
- **Micro-Action / Export Trigger:** Compact height (32px), `label-sm` font, incorporating tactile Lucide iconography (`Ship`, `Download`, `Scale`).

### 2. Commodity Ticker (High-Tech Bar)
- Full-width ticker pinned to top or sub-navigation.
- Dark obsidian base (`#081C16`) with high-contrast text. Displays real-time harvest spot pricing for export grades (e.g., *Cinnamon Vera AA*, *Clove Lalpari FAQ*, *Banda Nutmeg ABCD*).
- Price change chips feature soft pill styling with emerald (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.15)`) for gains and ochre for neutral/spot shifts, paired with micro sparkline graphs.

### 3. Glassmorphic Batch Trackers
- Encased within frosted glass container cards (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).
- Visual horizontal timeline featuring interactive nodes: *Harvest (Kerinci)* → *Sun-Curing* → *Lab Assay (VO Content & Moisture)* → *Phytosanitary Cleared* → *Port of Tanjung Priok* → *In Transit*.
- Completed steps feature `#0F2D25` fills with glowing amber active markers and dashed organic connecting vectors.

### 4. Interactive Quotation Calculator
- Multi-step configurator featuring large, tactile toggle buttons for Metric Tons (MT), Container Type (20ft FCL / 40ft FCL / LCL), Grade Assortments, and Incoterms (FOB, CIF, CFR).
- Dynamic pricing breakdown card with real-time currency conversion (USD, EUR, IDR) displayed in tabular Plus Jakarta Sans.

### 5. ESG & Organic Compliance Badges
- Hybrid pill-capsules featuring an earthy border (`1px solid rgba(15, 45, 37, 0.2)`), containing an SVG certified seal (Rainforest Alliance, USDA Organic, Fair Trade, Halal Indonesia).
- Tooltip hover displays plantation geo-coordinates, smallholder farmer cooperative split, and soil carbon index.

### 6. Input Fields & Data Selectors
- Background `#FDFBF7`, framed by subtle sandstone border (`#E6E0D2`).
- Focus state: border shifts directly to `#0F2D25` with a subtle spice-amber focus ring (`rgba(217, 119, 6, 0.25)`).
- Form inputs are paired with monospaced metadata guides (e.g., *HS-Code validation*, *Max Moisture: 12%*).

### 7. Product & Terroir Cards
- Vertical hierarchy: high-resolution botanical photography showcasing spice grain and cure grade, crowned with an origin tag (e.g., *Maluku Islands*, *Sumatra High Plains*).
- Lower body features an essential oil percentage gauge (e.g., *Min. Volatile Oil: 2.5%*), batch volume availability, and a direct "Add to Sample Manifest" trigger.
