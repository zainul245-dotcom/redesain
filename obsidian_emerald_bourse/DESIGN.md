## Brand & Style

The design system projects sovereign-grade authority, rare natural wealth, and high-frequency precision. Designed for ultra-high-net-worth buyers, sovereign food security syndicates, and institutional commodity brokers trading Indonesian spice harvests (Moluccan nutmeg, wild forest vanilla, Muntok white pepper, high-curcumin turmeric), the UI synthesizes high-velocity financial analytics with editorial heritage luxury.

The aesthetic direction merges **High-Density Financial Terminal** architecture (Refinitiv, Bloomberg) with **Bespoke Luxury Dark Glassmorphism**. True obsidian and black-jade surfaces form an infinite canvas where data glows like phosphorescent instrumentation. 

Key visual principles:
- **Obsidian Density:** Interfaces leverage pure OLED deep blacks paired with near-black mineral tints to establish extreme tonal gravity.
- **Controlled Luminescence:** Accent colors behave like calibrated backlight filaments—precise, focused, and purposeful rather than decorative.
- **Editorial Finery Meets Algorithmic Speed:** Regal, high-contrast serif headlines signal pedigree and agricultural legacy, while clinical monospaced counters deliver microsecond-accurate trade telemetry.

The palette is engineered specifically for HDR and OLED displays, maximizing dynamic range between deep black substrates and radiant phosphors.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements an intentional bifurcation of roles:
- **Playfair Display** provides high-society editorial gravitas, reserved exclusively for executive summaries, lot provenance narratives, commodity grade certifications, and major portal section headlines. It is rendered with high contrast against the deep black canvas.
- **Inter** provides high legibility for tabular documentation, legal trade contracts, navigation links, and conversational negotiation threads.
- **JetBrains Mono** governs the trading terminal reality: prices per metric ton, container BL numbers, moisture content percentages, volatile oil analysis, and international time stamps (WIB / UTC / EST).

All telemetry labels using `label-caps` must be rendered in full uppercase with expanded tracking to maintain legibility under high-density market monitor conditions.

## Layout & Spacing

The terminal uses a 16-column fluid desk layout on screens over 1600px (standard commodity floor resolution) and scales down to a structured 12-column layout on standard desktop viewports, 8 columns on tablet, and 4 columns on mobile.

Layout Rules:
- **Information Density:** Spacing between market telemetry cells uses tight spacing (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) to maximize visible lots per viewport.
- **Atmospheric Margins:** Outer frames and editorial provenance showcases introduce expansive breathing room using `space-xl` and `margin-desktop` to maintain an aura of exclusive luxury.
- **Desktop (1200px+):** Fixed telemetry ticker header (36px), persistent left rail navigation (collapsible from 240px to 64px), multi-pane split layout for real-time trade books and shipping lane tracking.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** Split-view cascades into swipeable or stacked analytics panes; ticker adapts to horizontal marquee.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked stream. Critical market rates pin to a fixed bottom trading bar.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established via **Specular Glassmorphism and Luminescent Radiance**, avoiding heavy opaque drop shadows that break true OLED blackness.

Visual tiers operate through optical transparency and phosphor backlighting:
- **Floor (Elevation 0):** Pure `#050706` background. Zero elevation. Completely non-reflective.
- **Tier 1 - Substrate Panels (`backdrop-blur-md`):** `rgba(9, 14, 12, 0.75)` with a structural hairline border of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`. Used for trading tables, lot lists, and chart enclosures.
- **Tier 2 - Focused Glass Cards:** `rgba(13, 20, 17, 0.85)` with ambient glowing borders: `rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.20)` blending into `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`. Inner edge highlights feature a 1px top stroke of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)` mimicking beveled obsidian glass.
- **Tier 3 - Floating Modals & Order Trays:** `rgba(13, 20, 17, 0.95)` with dual shadow structure: a deep drop shadow (`0 20px 40px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`) paired with an emerald peripheral glow (`0 0 30px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`).

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary uses the `Soft` standard (`0.25rem` / 4px base radius) to evoke the surgical precision of cutting-edge trading hardware and precision-cut gemstones.

- **Data Tables, Input Fields & Telemetry Badges:** Micro-radii (2px to 4px) to keep data boundaries sharp, architectural, and compact.
- **Luxury Cards & Analytical Panels:** Intermediate radius (`0.5rem` / 8px via `rounded-lg`) to balance the hard telemetry with modern software refinement.
- **Interactive Triggers (Pills):** Status indicators and high-level category pills may use fully rounded pill profiles (`9999px`) only when indicating state or commodity grade categorization (e.g., "Grade A Muntok", "SGS Inspected").

## Components

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary Execution Button (Buy Order / Lock Price):** Solid background of `#10b981` with black text (`#050706`, `font-weight: 600`), 4px border radius, and a subtle drop shadow (`0 0 16px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35)`). On hover, color transitions to `#34d399` with intensified neon halo.
- **Secondary Luxury Trigger (Request Allocation / Consignment Note):** Obsidian glass base (`#090e0c`) with a hairline border in `#f59e0b`, text in `#f59e0b`. Hovering reveals a radial golden amber glow (`0 0 20px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.20)`) and text brightens to `#fde68a`.
- **Tertiary Utility Actions:** Ghost styling with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.06)` border and `#94a3b8` text, shifting to `#f8fafc` on hover.

### Glassmorphic Trading Cards
- Constructed with `background: rgba(9, 14, 12, 0.85)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`, and an asymmetrical rim border: `border: 1px solid rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.20)` with a top edge accent of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.12)`.
- Cards housing live prices include an ambient micro-indicator in the top-right corner that pulses green on upward tick, amber on neutral holding, and red on downward tick.

### Financial Tickers & Telemetry Tables
- **Header:** JetBrains Mono, 10px uppercase, tracked wide (`0.1em`), muted slate color (`#64748b`).
- **Rows:** Alternate between transparent and `rgba(9, 14, 12, 0.40)`. Bottom borders are hairline `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.04)`.
- **Numerical Alignment:** Monospaced tabular lining numbers (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`), strictly right-aligned for volume and price indices.

### Status Badges & Commodity Seals
- **Vibrant Telemetry Badges:** Semi-translucent base with 100% saturated borders and text.
  - *Certified Organic / Export Ready:* `bg-[#10b981]/10 text-[#10b981] border-[#10b981]/30`.
  - *Customs Cleared / In Escrow:* `bg-[#f59e0b]/10 text-[#f59e0b] border-[#f59e0b]/30`.
  - *Vessel Delayed / Quarantine:* `bg-[#ef4444]/10 text-[#ef4444] border-[#ef4444]/30`.
- Badges use 2px border radius, JetBrains Mono font at 11px, with 4px vertical and 8px horizontal padding.

### Input Fields & Order Sliders
- Background: Inset Deep Black (`#030403`) with inset shadow (`inset 0 2px 4px rgba(0,0,0,0.8)`).
- Border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Focus State: Border snaps to `#10b981` with an immediate zero-spread outline glow of `0 0 0 1px #10b981, 0 0 12px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25)`.
- Numeric Steppers & Increments: Integrated JetBrains Mono buttons embedded seamlessly inside the input bounds.

### Specialized Commodity Components
- **Volatile Oil & Moisture Metric Gauges:** Radial or segmented micro-progress bars utilizing high-saturation emerald-to-amber gradient fills set within grooved obsidian tracks.
- **Vessel Tracking Lane:** Miniature schematic map module rendering dark maritime paths with luminescent emerald tracking pings representing live spice container freighters transiting between Tanjung Priok, Rotterdam, and Singapore.
